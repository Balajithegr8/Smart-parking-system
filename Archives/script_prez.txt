Imagine a scorching hot day as you head to SRM or another destination, only to find the parking lots full. Frustrating, isn't it? Especially during festivals or events, this can be a nightmare. To address this issue, we've developed an app that significantly eases parking woes and simplifies your life.

Now basic outline of our idea...

For users, we've created a mobile application allowing them to book or pre-book parking slots. For those who arrive without pre-booking, unaware of our app, we've developed a web application using the MERN stack. This tool will be provided to security guards stationed at each parking lot's entry/exit. They can manually book slots for customers and allocate them a spot. When customers return to retrieve their vehicles, they simply pay a small fee determined by our dynamic pricing algorithm fixed before the slot is booked, which utilizes machine learning and computer vision.

Of course, there are always rule-breakers. For those who park overtime, fines will be imposed to their accounts.

Advantages:

~ Security
~ Feasibility
~ Economic benefits (reducing carbon footprint and pollution)
~ Improved safety
~ Flexibility
~ Future-proofing
~ Enhanced customer experience
~ Scalability
